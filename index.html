GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Responsible analytics matters.

🔒 Protect privacy

Use data lawfully, minimise personal information and avoid exposing sensitive records.

⚖️ Address bias

Consider whether your approach treats different groups fairly.

🔍 Explain your results

Be transparent about assumptions, limitations and AI-assisted work.

Detailed competition rules, submission requirements, judging criteria and prizes will be communicated by the organisers. Do not submit confidential or identifiable personal data in your project materials.
